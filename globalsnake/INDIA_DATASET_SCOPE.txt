VenomOncoDB India-wide dataset scope

Goal: >=100 distinct, source-supported venom-derived anticancer peptides.
Geographic scope: India-wide, not a single state. Include relevant venomous animal groups.
Do not count general toxin records as anticancer peptides unless anticancer experimental evidence is source-supported.
Do not count multiple assays/publications for one peptide as multiple unique peptides.
The PubMed discovery script creates a candidate paper queue only; manual curation is required.
No fabricated or synthetic records are permitted.

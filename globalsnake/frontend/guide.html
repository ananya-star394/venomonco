USER GUIDE · LEARNING · RESEARCH

Use VenomOncoDB with confidence.

Choose the level of detail you need without changing the underlying evidence model.

🎓

For students

Start with Explorer. Open a peptide and learn how sequence, cancer-cell-line evidence, assay type, mechanism and structure connect.

  1. Search a peptide such as Chlorotoxin.
  2. Open its sequence analysis.
  3. Read the activity table by cell line.
  4. Inspect the evidence completeness profile.
  5. Open the 3D structure when a PDB link exists.
🔬

For researchers

Use the filters and evidence records to compare documented experiments without collapsing different studies into one value.

  • Trace PMID and source identifiers.
  • Compare cancer types and cell lines.
  • Inspect curation state and missing fields.
  • Use sequence similarity as a research aid, not as proof of shared function.
🩺

For doctors & clinical researchers

The clinical context layer distinguishes preclinical cell-line evidence from clinical evidence. It is designed for literature orientation, not patient-specific decisions.

A reported in-vitro activity does not establish human efficacy, safety, dosage or treatment suitability.
🌐

For general users

Use the plain-language summaries on peptide records and the Insights page. Each evidence statement is kept close to its source context.

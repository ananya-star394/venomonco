EVIDENCE-LED TOXIN RESEARCH

Toxicity Analysis Tool

Explore documented animal-toxin annotations and calculate basic properties of a submitted protein/peptide sequence. This tool does not generate a validated toxicity score or estimate how toxic a substance is to a person.

Interpretation note: sequence composition and similarity are not toxicity measurements. Toxicity depends on the molecule, dose, route, exposure, organism, target, and experimental conditions. Only source-supported annotations and measured values should be treated as toxicity evidence.

1. Analyse a sequence

Processing is performed in your browser; this page does not upload the sequence. Accepts the 20 standard amino-acid symbols.

2. Search toxin evidence records

Open UniProt Tox-Prot
Search your local imported toxin catalog. If no records are loaded yet, run the documented UniProt importer after installing the package.

3. Calculate measured peptide amount and concentration

First calculate the sequence properties above. Then enter experimentally measured sample amounts. These calculations describe the sample you measured; they do not estimate how much toxin exists in an animal's venom.

Venom abundance (%) can only be calculated if the toxin mass and total venom mass were measured from the same sample using a suitable quantitative method. Do not substitute sequence length, molecular weight or UniProt annotations for measured mass.

4. Record a source-reported toxicity result

Enter a result exactly as reported in a paper. This form does not generate toxicity measurements. Keep endpoint, units, test model and publication together.

LD₅₀, IC₅₀, LC₅₀, cell viability and haemolysis are different endpoints and must not be compared as equivalent measures.

What does “toxicity analysis” mean here?

Analysis layerOutputInterpretation
Sequence propertiesLength, approximate molecular mass, residue composition, hydrophobic fraction, simple side-chain countsCalculated descriptors only; not toxicity
Curated toxin annotationUniProt accession, organism, annotated function/tissue specificity, source linkEvidence as annotated by the source; inspect the entry and references
Experimental toxicityOnly if a source record reports an assay, endpoint, dose and test contextValues must remain tied to the exact experimental context
Similarity searchPotential sequence homologues from a reference databaseSimilarity does not prove function, potency or safety

The catalog importer selects reviewed animal toxin/venom annotations from UniProtKB. A source annotation is not automatically a quantitative toxicity measurement. Database records are separate from venom-derived anticancer peptide records unless an evidence-backed link is curated.

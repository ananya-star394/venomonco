Evidence rule: the interface distinguishes experimentally determined PDB structures from predicted models and from “no linked structure”. A structure hit is not automatically a verified match: sequence identity, chain/entity, construct length and experimental method must be reviewed. Batch structure verification queue · up to 100 sequences

Paste one or more FASTA records. The tool searches the RCSB Protein Data Bank sequence-search service, retrieves candidate polymer-entity sequences, compares the normalized sequence and reports an explicit identity/length result. It does not infer anticancer activity from a structure.

Interpretation: a 100% sequence identity hit is a strong sequence-match candidate, but you must still check that the deposited entity is the intended mature peptide/construct and that the entry is experimentally determined. Predicted AlphaFold models are not PDB experimental structures.

External research and evidence databases

These resources cover different evidence types. A record in an antimicrobial peptide database does not by itself establish anticancer activity. Keep endpoint, species, assay, sequence and citation separate.

CancerPPD2 ↗

Anticancer peptide/protein records, cell lines, assays, sequence and structure fields; source for candidate discovery.

RCSB Protein Data Bank ↗

Deposited macromolecular structures. Inspect entity sequence, chain, construct and experimental method.

UniProtKB ↗

Protein sequence, accession, organism and curated functional annotations; distinguish reviewed from unreviewed records.

PubMed ↗

Indexed biomedical literature. Follow each claim to the primary paper, assay and supplementary methods.

Europe PMC ↗

Literature, abstracts and open-access full-text links with a REST API for reproducible searches.

AlphaFold DB ↗

Predicted structures, not experimental PDB evidence. Check model confidence and whether the entry covers the peptide.

NCBI ↗

Sequence databases, BLAST, taxonomy and literature identifiers for sequence/source cross-checking.

Tox-Prot ↗

Animal toxin protein annotations and toxin-related nomenclature; not an anticancer assay database.

DBAASP ↗

Experimentally tested antimicrobial/cytotoxic peptide data. Endpoint context is essential; not a substitute for cancer-cell evidence.

DRAMP ↗

Antimicrobial peptide sequences, structures, activity and literature metadata.

ChEMBL ↗

Bioactivity records and assay metadata where available; check target, assay type and units before comparing.

ClinicalTrials.gov ↗

Registered studies. A search hit is not proof of current recruitment or clinical efficacy; inspect status and dates.

Methodology & Research Gap

A reproducible evidence workflow for venom-associated anticancer peptide research.

RESEARCH GAP

Fragmented evidence is not the same as connected evidence.

Large anticancer-peptide repositories already provide valuable peptide, origin, cancer-cell-line and structural records. CancerPPD2, for example, reports 6,521 entries covering 392 cancer cell lines and 28 tissue types. VenomOncoDB therefore does not claim novelty from simply storing anticancer peptides. Its project-level contribution is the venom-focused evidence workflow: connecting source organism, peptide sequence, cell-line experiment, mechanism evidence, structure and publication provenance in a single auditable interface.

01 · FragmentationSequence, experiment, mechanism, structure and publication data live in different evidence layers.
02 · Context lossDifferent cell lines and assays can be hidden when studies are reduced to one peptide-level score.
03 · Provenance gapUsers need to know where a value came from and whether it was curated or merely imported.
04 · Translation gapPreclinical cell-line observations should not be presented as human clinical efficacy.

1 · Candidate discovery

Candidate records may be discovered through CancerPPD/CancerPPD2, UniProt/Tox-Prot, RCSB PDB and primary publications. Discovery is not automatically treated as proof of venom origin or therapeutic relevance.

2 · Curation

Sequence, source organism, cancer type, cell line, assay, mechanism, structure and publication are checked as separate fields. Conflicting studies remain separate records.

3 · Quality control

Sequences are normalized and validated. Missing values remain NULL or “Not reported”. Source identifiers are retained.

4 · Bioinformatics layer

Sequence length, composition, cysteine count and hydrophobic fraction are computed directly from the sequence. Similarity can support exploration but does not establish shared anticancer activity, toxicity, selectivity or mechanism.

5 · Evidence engine

PHP REST endpoints expose search, record detail, summary and dashboard data to the frontend.

DATA GOVERNANCE
No fabricated IC50 values. No mechanism inference from sequence alone. No clinical efficacy prediction. No negative biological claim from missing evidence.

Source hierarchy

  1. Primary research paper
  2. Primary abstract where full text is unavailable
  3. UniProt / Tox-Prot sequence evidence
  4. RCSB PDB structure evidence
  5. CancerPPD/CancerPPD2 candidate discovery
  6. Review literature for context

Audit model

Imported records retain source identifiers, URLs, curation state and audit notes. This creates a review trail rather than a black-box database.

STRUCTURAL BIOINFORMATICS

Sequence → structure → visual evidence

When a peptide has a verified PDB identifier, the record can open an interactive 3D viewer. RCSB PDB provides web-based molecular visualization through Mol* and supports structure exploration, sequence annotations and interaction analysis. VenomOncoDB uses structure as a documented evidence layer rather than inferring anticancer function from a 3D image.

TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

Full-stack implementation

HTML/CSS/JS→PHP REST API→PDO prepared SQL→MySQL/MariaDB

Interactive charts use Chart.js and structure pages use 3D molecular visualization. The responsive interface is also PWA-ready for future APK packaging.

VERIFICATION BOUNDARY

Verified set before volume

The synchronizer imports only CancerPPD2 rows whose Origin field explicitly supports Snake, Scorpion, Spider or Cone snail classification. Unclassified rows are excluded from the biological dataset rather than used to reach a numeric target. This makes the displayed verified count auditable and prevents dataset size from becoming a substitute for evidence quality.

REPRODUCIBLE CURATION QUALITY CONTROL

Validation Lab

Capture individual experimental study claims, check record completeness, triage possible disagreements, and quantify agreement between two reviewers.

Research boundary: this is a local curation and quality-control tool, not a validated biological classifier. A flag identifies something for human review; it does not automatically prove a paper is wrong or that two studies conflict. Records are stored in this browser only and are not sent to MySQL.

1 · Study-level claim extraction

One row should represent one specific experimental result, not every result in a paper merged together.

No data is uploaded to a server.

2 · Data-quality gate

The gate checks whether key fields are present. It does not judge whether the biological conclusion is correct.

Add study records to see quality checks.

Interpretation rule
A missing field is “not documented in this record”, not proof that the experiment, mechanism, toxicity, or structure does not exist.

3 · Study register and potential discrepancy triage

Rows with the same candidate, cell model, assay, endpoint, and units but differing reported values are flagged for review. Differences may be legitimate if dose, exposure, controls, peptide form, or experimental conditions differ.

The discrepancy detector is a triage rule, not automatic contradiction resolution. Always return to the primary source and compare experimental conditions before deciding that findings conflict.

4 · Two-reviewer agreement

Cohen’s kappa is calculated from paired categorical labels in the saved study rows. It estimates agreement beyond chance for the current reviewed sample; it is unstable for very small or highly imbalanced samples.

Add records with reviewer labels to calculate agreement.

If only one reviewer has assessed a claim, do not interpret that row as independent agreement. Preserve disagreements for adjudication rather than silently choosing one label.

STUDENT & FACULTY TOOLKIT

Research Workbench

Sequence descriptors, toxicity-evidence triage, literature discovery, structure lookup, reproducible exports and a research-status worksheet—all in one place.

Scientific limitation: this page does not calculate a validated toxicity prediction or clinical safety score. Sequence descriptors are mathematical properties, not proof of toxicity, anticancer activity, selectivity or safety. Only label a molecule “anticancer” when a traceable experimental source supports that claim.

1. Peptide sequence calculator

Calculates approximate average mass, charge at pH 7.4, estimated pI, GRAVY, hydrophobic fraction, aromatic fraction, cysteine count and amino-acid composition. The pI/charge estimates use simplified side-chain pKa values.

2. Toxin evidence lookup

Search locally imported toxin annotations by toxin name, accession or organism. This lookup does not calculate venom abundance or toxicity from a sequence. For peptide molecular properties, measured sample concentration calculations and source-reported toxicity endpoints, open Toxicity Analysis.

Open Toxicity Analysis & Amount Calculator
Search requires the local toxin catalog to have been imported.

A toxin annotation is not a measured quantity. LD50, cell-viability IC50 and haemolysis are different endpoints. Enter source-reported measurements in Toxicity Analysis; never infer them from sequence or accession alone.

3. Literature & development tracker

Generate live search links for a candidate. Search results are discovery links, not proof that a project is currently active or completed.

Enter a candidate to create source-search links.
Status wording guide
“Experimental anticancer activity reported” = supported by a specific source. “Under investigation” = a traceable, dated study or registered project supports this. “No linked evidence in this database” = not yet curated here; it does not mean research has never started.

4. Structure & sequence resources

Search structure repositories and sequence tools. A PDB ID should only be added to a peptide record after checking the chain/entity sequence and whether the structure represents the same peptide or a related construct.

Search RCSB PDB and sequence resources.

The links open official external resources. This tool does not claim a structure exists until a matching entry is checked.

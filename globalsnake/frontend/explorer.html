How to read record status

Verification and curation

Verified means the venom-origin claim has been supported by the curated source record. Needs review / partially curated means evidence is incomplete or still being checked. Not established does not mean biologically inactive.

Structure and modelling

A linked PDB entry is not automatically an exact peptide match; inspect its sequence-match metadata. “No linked structure” means none is linked in this database, and “modelling status not recorded” means the platform cannot claim modelling is in progress or complete.

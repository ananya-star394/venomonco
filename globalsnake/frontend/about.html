ABOUT THE PLATFORM

VenomOncoDB

A provenance-aware evidence platform for exploring venom-associated peptide research in cancer models. The platform is built to make scientific context visible instead of compressing it into an unsupported therapeutic score.

THE PROBLEM

Evidence is fragmented

Sequence, origin, cancer-cell-line activity, mechanism, structure and literature often live in separate resources or are reported with different levels of detail.

THE APPROACH

Connect the evidence

VenomOncoDB links the layers into one record model while retaining the source identifier and curation state.

THE PRINCIPLE

Do not over-interpret

Missing information stays missing. A source-backed observation is not automatically a clinical conclusion.

PROJECT NOVELTY

Evidence Completeness Profile

The flagship concept is a transparent evidence profile across sequence, activity context, mechanism, structure and publication provenance. It is deliberately different from a “best peptide” or “anticancer score”: the platform reports what is documented and where evidence is incomplete.

Source→Sequence→Experiment→Mechanism→Structure→Publication
BOUNDARIES

What the platform does not claim.

  • It does not diagnose cancer.
  • It does not recommend a treatment or drug.
  • It does not predict clinical efficacy.
  • It does not convert missing evidence into a negative biological claim.
  • It does not treat sequence similarity as proof of shared mechanism or anticancer activity.
